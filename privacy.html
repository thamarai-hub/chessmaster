Who we are

ChessMaster (chessmaster.io) is operated by Shortcastle Technologies Private Limited (“we”, “us”, “our”). We are responsible for the personal data described in this policy.

This policy explains what we collect when you visit our website, register for a class or webinar, pay for a class, or contact us, and how we use it.

What we collect

We collect only what we need to run our classes and stay in touch with you:

  • Name of the person registering.
  • Email address.
  • WhatsApp number.
  • Country and state.
  • Children’s names, when a parent or guardian registers a child for a class.
  • Messages you send us, for example through the contact form, WhatsApp or email.
  • Payment confirmation details from our payment provider (see “Payments” below).

Our website also stores your preferred time zone in your browser so that class times show correctly. This stays on your device.

How we use it

  • To register you or your child for classes and webinars, and to send joining details.
  • To send class reminders, schedule changes and updates you asked for.
  • To answer your questions and provide support.
  • To process payments, issue receipts and keep accounting records required by law.
  • To keep our classes safe and prevent misuse.

We do not sell your personal data.

Children

Many of our students are under 18. A child must be registered by their parent or legal guardian, who gives consent on the child’s behalf. In that case we collect the child’s name together with the parent’s or guardian’s contact details, and we contact the parent or guardian, not the child.

If you believe a child’s details were given to us without a parent’s or guardian’s consent, email support@chessmaster.io and we will delete them.

Payments

Payments for paid classes and webinars are handled by our third-party payment provider. You enter your card, UPI or bank details on the payment provider’s secure page, not on our website, and we do not see or store those details.

The payment provider tells us whether a payment succeeded, the amount and a transaction reference, which we keep for receipts, refunds and our accounts. The payment provider handles your payment information under its own privacy policy.

WhatsApp messages

If you message us on WhatsApp, or ask to receive class updates on WhatsApp, those messages are sent through WhatsApp, a service of Meta Platforms. Meta processes them under its own terms and privacy policy.

You can stop receiving WhatsApp updates from us at any time by replying STOP. You can also email support@chessmaster.io and ask us to stop.

Google Fonts

Our website uses Google Fonts to display its text. When a page loads, your browser requests the fonts from Google’s servers, which means Google receives your IP address and basic browser information. Google handles this under its privacy policy.

Who we share it with

We share personal data only with the service providers we need to run ChessMaster, and only for the purposes above:

  • our payment provider, to take payments;
  • Meta (WhatsApp), to send and receive messages;
  • our email provider, to send and receive email;
  • our website hosting provider, which runs this website and keeps standard server logs.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Cookies and tracking

We do not currently use analytics, advertising cookies or tracking pixels on this website. If we start using them, we will update this policy first.

How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purposes above. Payment and invoice records are kept for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires. When data is no longer needed, we delete it.

Your rights

Under Indian data protection law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you or your child;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it;
  • withdraw your consent to messages or processing.

To do any of these, or to raise a concern or grievance, email support@chessmaster.io.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data. No method of sending or storing data online is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
